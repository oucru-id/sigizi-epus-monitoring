SIGIZI antenatal encounter completeness and service completion

Current analytical boundary

  • DAFTAR_IBU_HAMIL is pregnancy-registration evidence, not an ANC encounter.
  • Visit type is assigned from recorded gestational age, falling back to ANC date minus HPHT where available.
  • K1-K6 are non-overlapping gestational-age windows; they are not assigned by chronological row number.
  • Anaemia, pre-eclampsia, and KEK management are conditional components and are excluded from the headline denominator until eligibility rules are approved.
  • Facility-month results remain available in v_sigizi_anc_component_monitoring; the public overview uses a district rollup calculated before small-cell suppression.
